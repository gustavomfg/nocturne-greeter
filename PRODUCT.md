# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
Native Linux / Quickshell QML.

## Purpose and users
An in-session prototype of the NOCTURNE operating-system greeter, for the owner of this workstation. The job is a calm, legible transition from idle to authentication and then to a future desktop session.

## Confirmed constraints
The user delegates art direction. Preserve the state controller, fake authentication adapter, tests, reversible backups and low idle refresh. Never modify the display manager, PAM, boot, login services or execute power actions. No sudo or global dependency installation.

## Brand commitments
AMOLED black, restrained purple/lilac, cinematic orbital matter and a nearly black celestial body. Original vector identity, installed typefaces, functional keyboard and mouse affordances. Normal UI hides developer shortcuts.

## Scope
Redesign all visual and motion surfaces. Real authentication, user/session models and power integration remain future work. No new product claims.
